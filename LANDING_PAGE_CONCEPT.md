# Landing page concept: Enter Your Study World

## Promise

AcademiaZen turns scattered tasks, study material, focus time, and AI assistance into one calm daily system. The page demonstrates real workflows without testimonials, partner logos, or unsupported numerical claims.

## Narrative

1. **Chaos:** overlapping deadlines, loose notes, and context switching.
2. **Organization:** tasks and calendar converge into the next clear action.
3. **Focus:** a chosen task enters a quiet, recoverable focus session.
4. **Knowledge:** notes/PDFs become an organized library.
5. **Intelligence:** review generation and Zen AI operate on user-selected context with visible limits.
6. **Progress:** completed work and focus history provide honest feedback.
7. **Conversion:** create an account or open a product tour.

## Hero

Headline: **Enter your study world.** Supporting copy: **Plan what matters, focus without friction, and turn your own materials into useful review—inside one calm workspace.** Primary CTA: `Create your study space`. Secondary: `Explore the workflow`.

The visual is a semantic DOM study desk: task list, calendar card, timer, notebook, and Zen AI prompt arranged as an academic environment. It is useful and readable before enhancement. A lazy React Three Fiber scene may add controlled depth on capable devices only after the main content is interactive.

## Sections

Navigation; hero; one-system value proposition; three-step workflow; tasks/calendar; focus; knowledge library; review; Zen AI; privacy/trust; FAQ; final CTA; footer with privacy, terms, status, support, and sign-in.

## Performance/accessibility contract

No scroll hijacking or fake loader. Core copy and CTAs are HTML. 3D has a static image fallback, demand rendering, capped DPR, compressed assets, offscreen/tab pause, context-loss handling, reduced-motion bypass, mobile low-quality mode, and a separate public chunk. Authenticated routes never import Three.js. Landing remains navigable with JavaScript/3D unavailable.
